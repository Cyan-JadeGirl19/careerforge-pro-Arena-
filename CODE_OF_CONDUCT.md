# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in CareerForge Pro a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our Standards

Be kind. Be specific. Critique work, not people. Assume good faith.

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment or hostile, demeaning, or sexualized language
- Trolling or intentionally derailing discussions
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and will take
appropriate and fair corrective action for any behavior that is not
consistent with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and in public spaces when
an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.
